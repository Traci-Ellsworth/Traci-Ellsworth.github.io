## 🧑‍💻 About Me

I'm a statistics graduate student transitioning into data science with a focus on projects and roles that serve a clear public good. I bring with me 7 years of professional experience in two very different worlds: marketing and teaching.

**Before Data Science:**  
For 3 years as a digital marketing analyst at a start-up, I worked directly with the CMO and sales team on campaign strategy. I designed lead generation landing pages, and built an A/B testing system from scratch. I worked directly with the partners of several large, nationwide law firms to run their digital campaigns across several channels with 6-figure monthly budgets. 

Most recently, I worked for 4 years as a high school math teacher. This has been the most formative experience of my career, both personally and professionally. The skills I learned as a teacher include organization & time management, leadership & communication skills, adaptability, and communicating complex concepts to non-expert audiences. 

**What I'm Focused on Right Now:**
- Learning: Time Series Analysis, Categorical Data Analysis, Spatial Statistics, Bayesian Methods
- Building: End-to-end projects using datasets with a focus on public goods, including visualizations for non-technical audiences
- Practicing: SQL, Python, R, Tableau

**What I'm Looking For:** 
- Entry-level or junior data scientist or statistician roles
- Mission-driven organizations that seek to improve people's lives, such as in healthcare, education, public policy, environment, or other similar fields
- Roles in Tucson, AZ and/or remote work
